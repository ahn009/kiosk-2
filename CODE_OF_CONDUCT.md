# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and help maintain a safe community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the project or its community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
